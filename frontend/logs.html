Lucene Query Syntax

Boolean Operators

AND / &&
Both conditions must match
OR / ||
Either condition must match
NOT / !
Negates the condition

Field Queries

field:value
Exact match on field
field:"phrase"
Phrase match (contains)
field:val*
Wildcard (* = any chars, ? = single char)
field:/regex/
Regular expression match

Range Queries

[min TO max]
Inclusive range (includes min and max)
{min TO max}
Exclusive range (excludes min and max)
[min TO *]
Greater than or equal to min
[* TO max]
Less than or equal to max

Field Aliases

chain
chain_id (e.g., ethereum, polygon)
type
event_type (e.g., Transfer, Deposit)
address
contract_address, from_address, to_address
hash
tx_hash, block_hash
amount
amount, value fields
protocol
protocol, bridge_id

Examples (click to use)

chain:ethereum AND severity:critical
Critical events on Ethereum mainnet
event_type:Transfer AND amount:[1000 TO *]
Transfer events with amount >= 1000
(chain:ethereum OR chain:polygon) AND NOT severity:low
Non-low severity events on Ethereum or Polygon
address:0x* AND type:Mint
Mint events for any EVM address
protocol:"Wormhole" AND severity:[high TO critical]
High to critical Wormhole bridge events
chain:arbitrum AND block:[21000000 TO *]
Arbitrum events from block 21M onwards
